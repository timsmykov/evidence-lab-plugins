Evidence Lab preserves third-party authorship, provenance, and license terms
beside the packs that contain adapted material:

- `packs/core/evidence-lab-core/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
- `packs/domains/quantitative-sciences/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
- `packs/workflows/document-evidence/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`

- `packs/workflows/research-design/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
- `packs/workflows/structured-data-analysis/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`

Those notices identify the pinned upstream revision and the applicable MIT or
Apache-2.0 text stored in the pack's `LICENSES/` directory. When a pack is
copied or redistributed, keep its notices and license files with it.
