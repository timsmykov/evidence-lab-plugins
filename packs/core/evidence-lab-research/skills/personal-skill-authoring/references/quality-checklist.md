# Personal skill quality checklist

## Scope and trigger

- The skill has one reusable job and a concrete output.
- The description states when it activates and excludes adjacent one-off work.
- Five positive and three negative trigger examples cover realistic phrasing.

## Research safety

- Claims remain tied to sources or user-provided evidence.
- Human judgment points are explicit.
- The skill does not silently change data, fabricate results, or treat a model
  suggestion as a scientific finding.
- Sensitive, project-specific, and credential material is not embedded.

## Reproducibility

- Inputs, outputs, decision rules, and acceptance checks are visible.
- Repeated parsing, conversion, validation, and calculation use deterministic
  scripts when practical.
- Scripts fail clearly and do not overwrite an existing artifact by default.

## Portability

- The research method is not coupled to a single host.
- Codex and Claude Code differences are isolated in installation adapters.
- References and assets use relative paths inside the skill.

## Evidence of readiness

- Structure validation passes.
- Trigger evaluation passes.
- At least one representative end-to-end case has been inspected.
- Complex or consequential skills receive independent domain review before a
  production label.
