# evidence-lab-meeting-capture

This optional Evidence Lab operations pack turns meeting transcripts and rough notes into source-bounded summaries, then stores the finished page in the canonical Notion meeting register. It exists for the Evidence Lab team and is not part of the default researcher installation.

## Who it is for

Evidence Lab operators who document team meetings, course discussions, consulting planning, or product decisions.

## What it gives you

| Skill | Output |
|---|---|
| `evidence-lab-meeting-capture` | A structured meeting summary and a verified record in the canonical Notion register. |

## What it does not do

It does not replace the transcript, infer missing participants, create tasks without board intake, or mark a summary as verified before it has been checked against the source. It does not install for ordinary researchers by default.

The host-neutral contract is `pack.json`; Claude Code and Codex manifests are generated from it.

## Provenance

Derived from the Evidence Lab meeting-note migration and read-back verification completed on 2026-08-28. See `meta.json`.
