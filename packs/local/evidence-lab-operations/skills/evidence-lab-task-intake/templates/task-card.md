# Task card body

## Result

One observable outcome or artifact.

## Context

Why this task exists, which initiative it advances, and the source of the request.

## Actions

- Concrete action.
- Concrete action.

## Acceptance criteria

- Observable check.
- Artifact, evidence, or decision is linked.

## Constraints and dependencies

- Known constraint or `None`.
- Linked prerequisite or `None`.

For an XS task, compress this structure without deleting the result or acceptance criteria. Render all headings and prose in the selected card language.
